# Shopify App Listing - Pricing Configuration

## Issue 4.2.1 - Pricing Information

The app listing must include ALL pricing options including the free plan.

## Pricing Plans to Add in Partner Dashboard

### Free Plan
- **Plan Name:** Free
- **Price:** $0.00 USD
- **Billing Interval:** N/A (Free)
- **Trial Period:** None
- **Features:**
  - Scan up to 5 images per month
  - Basic optimization
  - Manual sync

### Basic Plan
- **Plan Name:** Basic
- **Price:** $9.99 USD
- **Billing Interval:** Every 30 days
- **Trial Period:** 7 days (optional)
- **Features:**
  - Scan up to 500 images per month
  - Advanced optimization
  - Auto-sync to Shopify
  - Priority support

### Pro Plan
- **Plan Name:** Pro
- **Price:** $29.99 USD
- **Billing Interval:** Every 30 days
- **Trial Period:** 7 days (optional)
- **Features:**
  - Unlimited images
  - Advanced optimization
  - Auto-sync to Shopify
  - Priority support
  - Bulk operations

## How to Update in Shopify Partner Dashboard

1. Go to Shopify Partner Dashboard
2. Select "Shopimage" app
3. Navigate to "App listing" → "Pricing"
4. Click "Add pricing plan"
5. Add all three plans above with exact details
6. Save changes
